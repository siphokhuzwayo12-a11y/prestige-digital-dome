The Novice Guide to Hydroponic Welfare: Transparent Nursery Shield

Lesson 1: Nutrient Lockout Vulnerability

In typical crop systems, substrate pH shifts go unnoticed until plants suffer from nitrogen or phosphorus deficiency (Nutrient Lockout). By housing the crops inside our high-transparency nursery, we manage microclimates and root moisture tension via digital sensory triggers.

This digital CMOS hardware edge automation prevents physical crop decay under severe environmental changes.

Lesson 2: Cascaded Chemical Safety

The nursery controller implements absolute safeguard execution via two cascaded logical checks:

  • Stage 1 (Nutrient Pump): Recirculation pumps activate if there is a moisture/nutrient deficit ($A$) OR pH drift ($B$), provided the main storage reserve is clear ($C$): $$\text{Y\_PUMP} = (A \lor B) \land C$$
  • Stage 2 (Dosing Solenoid): Corrective dosing valves open ONLY if the pH is verified out of bounds ($B$) AND active nutrient flow ($Y_{\text{PUMP}}$) is verified running: $$\text{Y\_DOSING} = B \land \text{Y\_PUMP}$$

Lesson 3: Concentration Shock Guard

In traditional automated setups, dosing acid directly into a stagnant pipe shocks the roots with toxic localized acidic blocks. Because our dosing logic is structurally coupled ($Y_{\text{DOSING}} = B \land Y_{\text{PUMP}}$), pH modifiers can physically only dispense when water is actively circulating.

If the water level falls ($C = 0$), the entire loop shuts down, preventing pump burnout and chemical oversaturation.
