Core Pedagogical Objective
DEMAND ↔ RECOVERY ↔ TRANSPORT

Master how system demand (Q_required) is balanced against CHP recovery (Q_recovered) and transported via engineered mass flow rate (V_dot).

Pillar 01 | Thermal Demand

1. Heat Requirement Calculation

Q_required = m_dot × C_p × ΔT

Step-By-Step Procedure

  • Identify the fluid mass flow rate (m_dot).
  • Use the specific heat capacity (C_p) of the circulating fluid.
  • Multiply by the target operational temperature differential (ΔT).

Engineering Example Baseline

• Mass Flow Rate (m_dot): 1 kg/s
• Specific Heat (C_p): 4.184 kJ/(kg·K)
• Temperature Delta (ΔT): 10 °C
Q_required = 1 × 4.184 × 10
Q_required = 41.84 kW
Pillar 02 | Energy Generation

2. CHP Heat Extraction Rate

Q_recovered = Q_exhaust (55%) + Q_coolant (35%)

Step-By-Step Procedure

  • Measure total exhaust heat output from co-generator.
  • Calculate usable high-grade exhaust thermal energy (55% factor).
  • Measure engine jacket coolant heat output.
  • Calculate usable low-grade coolant thermal energy (35% factor).
  • Sum recovered outputs for total primary thermal loop availability.

Co-Gen Unit Recovery Example

• Exhaust Output = 20 kW ➔ 20 × 0.55 = 11.0 kW
• Coolant Output = 21.5 kW ➔ 21.5 × 0.35 = 7.5 kW
Q_recovered = 11.0 kW + 7.5 kW
Q_recovered = 18.5 kW_thermal
Pillar 03 | Fluid Transport

3. Manifold Flow Rate Engineering

V_dot = Q_recovered / [ ρ × C_p × (T_supply - T_return) ]

Step-By-Step Procedure

  • Take total recovered thermal energy (Q_recovered) in Watts.
  • Multiply fluid density (ρ) × specific heat (C_p) × temperature differential (ΔT).
  • Divide total heat energy by this thermal transport product.
  • Convert output from m³/s to Liters per second (L/s) or m³/h.

Hydraulic Flow Calculation

• Q_recovered = 18,500 W, ρ = 1000 kg/m³
• C_p = 4184 J/(kg·K), ΔT = 10 °C (60°C - 50°C)
• V_dot = 18500 / (1000 × 4184 × 10)
V_dot ≈ 0.00044 m³/s
V_dot = 0.44 L/s (1.59 m³/h)
