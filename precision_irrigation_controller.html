The Novice Guide to Hydrological Sovereignty: Precision Water-Saving Shield

Lesson 1: Smart Microclimate Shading

In typical crop setups, unshaded ground leads to rapid soil drying and hyper-evaporation under severe Mpumalanga conditions. By shielding the crops under circular solar array shading structures, we drop crop transpiration demand by up to $30\%$.

This physical edge microchip automates borehole delivery pressure and saves electrical power.

Lesson 2: Cascaded Boolean Safeguards

The controller executes high-performance protection using two stages of Boolean logic:

  • Stage 1 (Solar Pump): Solar pumps trigger if there is Dry Soil ($A$) OR peak solar radiation ($B$), provided the reservoir tank clearance ($C$) is safe: $$\text{Y\_PUMP} = (A \lor B) \land C$$
  • Stage 2 (Drip Solenoid): Irrigation valves open ONLY if the Soil Moisture Deficit ($A$) is verified AND water pressure line ($Y_{\text{PUMP}}$) is active: $$\text{Y\_DRIP} = A \land \text{Y\_PUMP}$$

Lesson 3: The Dry Run Safeguard

During periods of empty reserve tanks ($C = 0$), the Borehole Safe sensor transitions to ground level. This deactivates both output gates instantly, protecting our brushless DC pump motors from catastrophic dry-run friction burnout.

By integrating physical protection parameters directly into the logic hardware, we safeguard biological yields.
