Module 01 // Biological Operating Parameters

Mesophilic Thermal Maintenance (37°C Standard)

  • Thermal Target: Core slurry temperature is regulated strictly at 37°C (±1°C) to optimize methanogenic bacterial activity.
  • Hydronic Heat Duty: Heat loss to ambient conditions (Secunda-Kinross Corridor) is offset via CHP engine jacket heat recovered through the primary manifold.
  • pH & Volatile Fatty Acids (VFA): Optimal pH range maintained between 6.8 and 7.4. Rapid drops indicate acidification risk requiring feed rate adjustment.
Module 02 // Biogas Scrubbing & Conditioning

Biogas Composition & Quality Enhancement

  • Gas Composition Target: 55–65% Methane (CH4), 35–45% Carbon Dioxide (CO2), <200 ppm Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S).
  • H2S Removal: Biogas passes through iron-sponge or media-filled desulfurization scrubbers to prevent corrosion in the CHP internal combustion engine.
  • Moisture Management: Condensation traps at low points on the gas line remove moisture prior to entering the generator fuel intake.
Module 03 // Safety Interlocks & SCADA Telemetry

Dome Pressure & Environmental Safety Protocol

  • PT-301 Pressure Monitoring: Continuous pressure sampling. Normal operating range: 10–25 mbar.
  • Overpressure Flare Bypass: Automated safety relief valve routes excess gas to thermal oxidizer/flare if vessel pressure exceeds 35 mbar.
  • Atmospheric Gas Detection: Dual CH4/H2S ambient sniffers integrated around the digester dome to trigger immediate SCADA alert and ventilation locks upon leak detection.
