Gravity Slurry Topography

Eliminating Mechanical Pumps

Pumping raw hog manure draws heavy electrical currents and invites constant mechanical blockages. We use earth physics to bypass this bottleneck entirely.

  • The 1-in-50 Gradient Rule: All sub-slat concrete flush channels are poured with a strict 2% drop toward the primary collection sump.
  • Scrape-Free Separation: Moisture and solid organic fractions move continuously via hydrostatic gravity displacement.

Digester Intake Coupling

The collection sump sits at the lowest topographical point on site, allowing automated hydraulic head pressure to push slurry directly into the digester intake valve.

The Thermal Balancing Logic

Prioritizing Energy Allocation

Our automation system applies strict hierarchical logic to handle environmental loads without over-stressing structural energy boundaries.

  • Primary Heat Load: Handled entirely by the biogas-driven sub-surface radiant steam lines during cold nights.
