1. Thermal Strategy & Objective

CONCEPT
  • Primary Goal: Maintain precise brooding temperatures across 4 distinct age-staged pods without expensive fossil LPG or electrical heating element grid reliance.
  • Energy Source: 65.0°C Hot Water Jacket Loop recovered directly from the CHP Hydronic Engine.
  • Value Proposition: Achieves 100% thermal displacement during critical 30-day chick brooding cycles.

2. Staged Thermal Gradation Matrix

SPECIFICATION
  • POD 01 (Week 1 - Day-Old Chicks): Setpoint = 33.5 °C (Critical warmth, high flow duty).
  • POD 02 (Week 2): Setpoint = 30.0 °C (Feather development phase).
  • POD 03 (Week 3): Setpoint = 27.0 °C (Intermediate growth phase).
  • POD 04 (Week 4+): Setpoint = 24.0 °C (Stabilized ambient maintenance).

3. Hydronic Control & SCADA Modulation

AUTOMATION
  • Each pod is equipped with an independent 2-Way Modulating Thermostatic Valve.
  • Temperature sensors continuously feed ambient & floor readings back to the Central SCADA.
  • Valves auto-throttle flow to keep water returns above 45.0 °C, returning optimal delta-T to the CHP heat exchanger.

4. Operational Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

OPERATIONS
  • Pre-warm Phase: Engage hydronic circulation 4 hours prior to chick placement until air/bedding reaches target.
  • Daily Verification: Confirm supply header temperature is maintained at $\ge 60.0 ^\circ\text{C}$.
  • Emergency Override: In case of CHP maintenance, BESS storage drives backup inline booster circulation.
