Stage 1: Suckling / Creep Phase

Age: 0 to 3–4 Weeks | Target Weight: Up to 7–8 kg

Nutrition & Feed

  • Feed Type: Sow Milk + Highly digestible Creep Feed (Pellets/Crumble).
  • Daily Quantity: Starts at 10–50 g/pig/day, scaling up to 150–200 g/day.
  • Estimated Cost: ~$1.20 – $1.80 / kg of feed.

Health & Vaccination

  • Day 1–3: Iron injection (100–200 mg) & tail docking/castration (if applicable).
  • Day 7–14: Mycoplasma hyopneumoniae (1st dose) & PCV2 (Porcine Circovirus).
  • Water: Ensure fresh water is available next to creep feed.

Welfare & Environment

  • Temperature: Heat lamp zone at 30°C–32°C for piglets; sow zone at 18°C–20°C.
  • Bedding/Flooring: Dry, non-slip mats under heat lamp to prevent crushing.
  • Care: Check for colostrum uptake within first 6 hours.

*Note: Feed costs vary by local market conditions; values shown represent average commercial estimates.

Stage 2: Weaner / Nursery Phase

Age: 4 to 10 Weeks | Target Weight: 8 kg to 25–30 kg

Nutrition & Feed

  • Feed Type: Starter I & II Rations (High protein, enriched amino acids).
  • Daily Quantity: 0.4 kg to 1.2 kg / pig / day.
  • Estimated Cost: ~$0.55 – $0.75 / kg of feed.

Health & Vaccination

  • Week 4–5: Booster for Mycoplasma & PCV2 (if 2-dose protocol).
  • Week 6: Erysipelas & PRRS (Porcine Reproductive and Respiratory Syndrome) if endemic.
  • Monitoring: Watch closely for Post-Weaning Diarrhea (PWD) and Strep. suis.

Welfare & Environment

  • Temperature: Weaning start at 26°C–28°C, gradually lower to 22°C by Week 10.
  • Group Dynamics: Group by uniform weight; maintain low stocking density (0.3 m²/pig).
  • Enrichment: Provide chewable toys (chains, wood blocks, suspended ropes).

*Note: Transition feeds smoothly over 3–5 days to prevent digestive stress.

Stage 3: Grower Phase

Age: 10 to 16 Weeks | Target Weight: 30 kg to 60–65 kg

Nutrition & Feed

  • Feed Type: Grower Ration (Standard grain/soybean meal mash or pellets).
  • Daily Quantity: 1.5 kg to 2.2 kg / pig / day.
  • Estimated Cost: ~$0.38 – $0.48 / kg of feed.

Health & Vaccination

  • Internal Parasites: Deworming via feed/water around Week 10–12.
  • Boosters: Actinobacillus pleuropneumoniae (APP) or Lawsonia if indicated by vet.
  • Biosecurity: Maintain strict footbaths and vermin control.

Welfare & Environment

  • Temperature: Target ambient range: 18°C–20°C.
  • Space: Minimum space requirement increases to 0.55–0.65 m²/pig.
  • Ventilation: High airflow needed to manage humidity and ammonia (<10 ppm).

*Note: Rapid muscular growth occurs in this phase; maintain balanced lysine-to-energy ratios.

Stage 4: Finisher Phase

Age: 16 to 22–24 Weeks | Target Weight: 65 kg to 100–115 kg

Nutrition & Feed

  • Feed Type: Finisher Ration (Lower protein, higher energy ratio).
  • Daily Quantity: 2.5 kg to 3.5 kg / pig / day.
  • Estimated Cost: ~$0.32 – $0.42 / kg of feed.

Health & Withdrawal

  • Withdrawal Periods: Mandatory clearance for ALL medications/feed additives before slaughter (usually 14–28 days).
  • Health Monitoring: Daily checks for respiratory issues or lameness.

Welfare & Environment

  • Temperature: Ideal range: 15°C–18°C (Watch for heat stress above 24°C).
  • Space: Minimum space allocation: 0.75–0.90 m²/pig.
  • Cooling: Utilize sprinklers/drip systems in warm climates.

*Note: Finisher feed accounts for approximately 60–65% of total lifetime feed costs.

Stage 5: Market Dispatch & Transport

Target Weight: 110–125 kg | Final Preparation

Pre-Sale Preparation

  • Feed Withdrawal: Fast pigs for 12–18 hours prior to slaughter (improves meat safety & cuts waste). Continuous water access required.
  • Documentation: Verify chain-of-custody, medication withdrawal logs, and health certificates.

Loading & Handling

  • Handling: Use soundboard/paddles only. Never use electric prods. Move in small groups (3–5 pigs).
  • Ramp Design: Non-slip flooring with an incline angle under 20 degrees.

Transport Welfare

  • Density: Provide approx 0.4–0.5 m² per 100 kg pig during transit.
  • Climate Control: Schedule transport for early morning/night during hot weather. Bedding (straw/sawdust) based on season.

*Note: Minimizing pre-slaughter stress directly prevents Pale, Soft, and Exudative (PSE) meat defects.
