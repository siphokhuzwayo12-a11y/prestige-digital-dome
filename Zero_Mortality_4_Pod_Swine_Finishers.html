Zero-Mortality Execution Protocol (7kg Weaner → 70kg Dispatch)
1. Sub-Pen Partitioning & Weight Classing
Split each 96 m² pod into two 48 m² sub-pens holding 20 pigs each. Sorting pigs by weight at entry stops bigger pigs from bullying smaller 7 kg piglets away from food and water.
2. Sub-Floor Thermal Protection
Keep floor temperatures steady at 28°C to 30°C for the first 14 days using hot water pipes under the concrete. This prevents cold drafts and stops piglets from piling on top of each other and suffocating.
3. Spatial Density & Zero-Congestion
Every pig receives 1.9 m² of space at 70 kg dispatch size—nearly three times the industry standard of 0.65 m². This eliminates crowding, reduces stress, and stops tail-biting entirely.
4. Slurry & Gas Elimination
A 2% floor slope drains manure continuously out of the barn into the biothermal digester. This keeps toxic ammonia gas below 10 ppm, protecting the pigs' lung health.
