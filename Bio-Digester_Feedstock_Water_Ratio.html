Ratio Breakdown — Phase A

  • Unmatched Reliability: Protects anaerobic methanogens from ammonia toxicity while avoiding solid crust formation.
  • Low-Maintenance Automation: Ideal for autonomous SCADA PLC valve scheduling without manual slurry intervention.
