Lesson 1: The Sovereign Objective

In livestock projects (such as 1,920-broiler batches or 160-porcine cycles), standard heaters fail during South African grid failures (Load Shedding). Rapid thermal shifts cause mortality.

Our objective is absolute temperature protection ($T \approx 30^\circ\text{C}$ for broilers) completely independent of the national grid.

Lesson 2: The Logical Guardians

We use three environmental inputs represented as binary values:

  • Input $A$: Solar energy harvesting level.
  • Input $B$: Active generator output ($Y_{GEN}$).
  • Input $C$: Cold threshold trigger ($T \le 2^\circ\text{C}$).

The ultimate safeguard is computed by the Boolean master formula: $$\text{Y\_HEAT} = (A \lor B) \land C$$

Lesson 3: The Actuator (Hydronic Valve)

When the output valve $Y_{HEAT}$ fires ($1$), it opens motorized PEX valves. This floods the underfloor concrete "thermal battery" with heat derived from the Biogas generator's water jacket.

By utilizing the structural thermal mass of concrete, we maintain heat for up to 14 hours without active fuel burning.
