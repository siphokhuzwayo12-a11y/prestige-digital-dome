Module 01 // Executive Summary

System Architecture & Overview

  • Core Mandate: Delivers resilient, uninterruptible power to critical Bio-Solar microgrid operations, including digester heating telemetry and corridor loads.
  • PV Capacity: 30kW DC monocrystalline string array providing high-efficiency solar harvesting.
  • BESS Capacity: 60kWh Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) storage operating at 48V nominal DC link.
  • Grid Interfacing: 30kW Hybrid PCS with Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) enabling seamless islanding within <20ms during grid loss.
Module 02 // Dispatch & Strategy

Operational Energy Flow & Dispatch Logic

  • Mode A (Solar Priority): Direct PV consumption powers site loads while remaining power tops up the 60kWh BESS bank.
  • Mode B (Peak Shaving & Backup): During solar deficits, BESS discharges smoothly to maintain zero dropouts on critical SCADA and heating circuits.
  • Mode C (CHP Co-Generation Interlock): Excess biogas thermal output from the 70m³ digester loop is dynamically balanced alongside electrical state-of-charge (SoC).
  • Target DoD: System is programmatically locked to an 80% Depth of Discharge to maximize LFP cell lifecycles.
Module 03 // SCADA & Protection

Instrumentation, Control & Telemetry Protocols

  • BMS-1 Master Controller: Real-time cell balancing, thermal monitoring, and individual module State-of-Charge tracking.
  • EM-201 & PM-201 Meters: Continuous SCADA sampling of power factor, harmonic distortion, and net power export/import vectors.
  • Safety Interlocks: Multi-stage DC over-current break switch, temperature-actuated isolators, and automated ATS trip protocols.
