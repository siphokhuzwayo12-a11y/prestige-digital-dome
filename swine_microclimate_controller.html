The Novice Guide to Swine Logic Systems: Swine Heat Stress Shield

Lesson 1: Swine Thermal Inability

Large White swine (specifically heavy **Finisher pigs** inside your **160-head cohort**) do not possess functional sweat glands. Because they cannot sweat, evaporative cooling *must* be facilitated artificially via skin-wetting drip lines and concurrent high-velocity airflow.

This controller dynamically prevents respiratory failure and cardiac arrest from heat stress.

Lesson 2: Integrated Logical Safeguards

The controller executes high-performance protection using two stages of Boolean logic:

  • Stage 1 (Ventilation): Fans activate if there is Heat ($A$) OR Toxicity ($B$), AND the safety override ($C$) is clear: $$\text{Y\_FAN} = (A \lor B) \land C$$
  • Stage 2 (Drip-Wetting): Drip valves open ONLY if the High Temperature sensor ($A$) is active AND active ventilation ($Y_{\text{FAN}}$) is confirmed running: $$\text{Y\_DRIP} = A \land \text{Y\_FAN}$$

Lesson 3: The Cold Draft Guard

During cold Mpumalanga winter nights ($T \le 16^\circ\text{C}$), the Cold Draft sensor ($C_{\text{SAFE}}$) transitions to $0$. This deactivates the AND gates, locking out both fans and water drippers immediately to protect vulnerable swine from pneumonia and dynamic shock.

By integrating physical protection parameters directly into the logic hardware, we safeguard biological yields.
