02. Thermal Manifold Sizing & Heat Transfer Engineering

[THERMAL ENERGY BALANCING FORMULAS]
• Heat Requirement: Q_required = m_dot * C_p * ΔT
• CHP Heat Extraction Rate: Q_recovered = Q_exhaust (55%) + Q_coolant (35%) = ~18.5 kW_thermal
• Manifold Flow Rate: V_dot = Q_recovered / (ρ * C_p * (T_supply - T_return))
• Parameter Values: ρ = 1000 kg/m³, C_p = 4.184 kJ/kg·K, ΔT_manifold = 10°C (60°C Supply / 50°C Return)

Hydronic Manifold Calculations

  • Recovered Thermal Output: 18.5 kW thermal continuous during CHP runtime cycle.
  • Primary Loop Flow Rate: V_dot = 18.5 kW / (4.184 × 10) = 0.442 L/s (1.59 m³/h).
  • Header Pipe Sizing: DN32 Stainless Steel Header (Velocity = 0.52 m/s, maintaining laminar flow).
  • Nursery Sub-Floor Loop: 16mm PEX-a piping @ 150mm spacing embedded in 75mm concrete slab.
  • Secondary Mixing Valve (TV-101): Modulates 60°C primary water down to 35°C floor supply.

Solid Fertilizer Production Engine

  • Daily Digestate Discharge: 1.75 m³/day wet digestate (8% Total Solids).
  • Mechanical Dewatering: Screw press separator compresses digestate to 25% Total Solids (TS).
  • Solid Output Yield: ~560 kg/day moist bio-fertilizer cake.
  • Solar Thermal Curing Shed: Passive drying to 85% dry matter (165 kg/day bagged product).
  • Nutrient Analysis: N-P-K ratio of 3.2% - 2.1% - 2.4% + rich humic bio-active organic carbon.
